# Theme

## Compact token summary

- Primary: warm amber/orange `--primary`; soft neutral page background `#eef2f2`; white surface cards; muted slate text.
- Presentation: rounded cards and controls, dark outline/shadow accents, dense but breathable dashboard grids.
- Primary UI: filled amber buttons; secondary white/neutral buttons; status pills for success and role state.
- Typography: system sans-serif, bold compact headings, large price values.
- Breakpoints: 900px collapses four/three-column grids to two; 650px collapses to single-column.

## Source locations

- Full primary stylesheet: `frontend/css/style.css`
- Responsive overrides: `frontend/css/responsive.css`

The target must retain this established visual language; requested work adds workflow clarity, not a brand restyle.
